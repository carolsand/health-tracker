Your Week-by-Week Plan

Check off each session as you complete it. Weeks keep going past week 8 — the plan extends automatically, and you can add more with the button at the bottom.

Progressive overload rule: when all rounds and reps feel easy with good form, increase the weight at your next session. Your equipment: Rogue rack & barbell, dumbbells, kettlebells, leg machine, stationary bike, 12-lb med ball, bands, foam rollers.
🦵 Knee-recovery rules (apply to every session): warm up on the bike first · work only in a pain-free range · box squats limit your depth — use them · skip leg extensions for now · never foam-roll the knee joint itself · mild muscle soreness is fine, sharp or swelling-type knee pain means stop. Have your doctor or physical therapist confirm this plan fits your specific injury.
